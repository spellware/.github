# SpellWare

SpellWare provides software engineering and community management for developer-facing companies. One accountable team, from the first moderation rule to the last line of code, so your own team can stay focused on the product.

Founded by [Naomi Carrigan](https://spellware.dev/team/naomi-carrigan/). Formerly known as NHCarrigan.

## What we do

| Service | In short |
| --- | --- |
| [Community Operations](https://spellware.dev/services/community-operations/) | The day-to-day running of your community, so your team can stay focused on the product. |
| [Open Source and Developer Experience](https://spellware.dev/services/open-source-developer-experience/) | Engineering capacity for the projects and tools your developers depend on. |
| [Community Strategy](https://spellware.dev/services/community-strategy/) | A fixed-scope engagement that gives your community structure, policy and direction. |
| [Custom Software](https://spellware.dev/services/custom-software/) | Purpose-built tools, scoped and delivered against clear milestones. |
| [Codebase Cleanup](https://spellware.dev/services/codebase-cleanup/) | A one-off refactor of a codebase that was built quickly, so it is easier to maintain and safer to run. |
| [Professional Mentorship](https://spellware.dev/services/professional-mentorship/) | Structured mentorship for individuals building a career, and onboarding programmes for companies growing a team. |

See [our past work](https://spellware.dev/work/) and [the questions we are asked most](https://spellware.dev/faq/).

## Work with us

Tell us what you need through [our project enquiry form](https://mktg.spellware.dev/hire), or write to [contact@spellware.dev](mailto:contact@spellware.dev). We typically reply within 3 to 5 business days. If it looks like a fit, we arrange an introductory call ourselves, and we do not charge for it.

## Open source in this organisation

- **Shared tooling:** [eslint-config](https://github.com/spellware/eslint-config), [typescript-config](https://github.com/spellware/typescript-config), [logger](https://github.com/spellware/logger) and [discord-analytics](https://github.com/spellware/discord-analytics). They are published on npm under the `@nhcarrigan` scope.
- **Build and maintenance tools:** [website-headers](https://github.com/spellware/website-headers), which generates the shared script our sites load, [minori](https://github.com/spellware/minori), an automated dependency update manager, and [template](https://github.com/spellware/template), our standard community files, issue forms and workflows.
- **Discord applications we run:** [Callista](https://github.com/spellware/callista), [Gwen Abalise](https://github.com/spellware/gwen-abalise), [Liora](https://github.com/spellware/liora), [Pavelle](https://github.com/spellware/pavelle), [Sorielle](https://github.com/spellware/sorielle) and [Umbrelle](https://github.com/spellware/umbrelle). Each has a page in [our product directory](https://spellware.dev/products/).
- **For the community:** [pink-tech](https://github.com/spellware/pink-tech), a directory of inspiring women leaders, innovators and experts in technology.

Each repository states its own licence. Our [contributing guide](https://docs.spellware.dev/dev/contributing/) explains how to take part.
